# Brief template

Write the brief in the user's language. Keep every `Moves the decision by:`
line — it is the record of why the section survived, and it is what makes the
next brief faster to write.

```markdown
---
title: <proposal title>
created: YYYY-MM-DD
type: note
tags: []
---

# <proposal title>

## The decision

<one sentence: who decides whether/which what, by when>

Decider: <name or role>
Meeting: <date, duration, attendees>

## The fence — not decided here

- <out of scope item>
- <out of scope item>

## Background

Moves the decision by: <one line>

<the problem, and why now rather than later>

## The proposal

Moves the decision by: <one line>

<what is proposed>

Diagram: <what the diagram must show — the before/after, the flow, or the
structure. One diagram, one idea.>

## Alternatives

Moves the decision by: <one line>

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| <alternative> | <reason rejected> |
| Do nothing | <what it costs> |

## Impact

Moves the decision by: <one line>

- Cost / effort: <...>
- Risk: <...>
- If it goes wrong: <...>

## The ask

<the decision restated verbatim>

Next action: <action> — <owner> — <when>

## Appendix — anticipated Q&A

**Q. <question as the room would ask it>**
A. <the answer the user can actually give>

## Open questions

- <objection from grilling with no answer yet>
```
