# My rules

What you will not do, and how you behave when it is inconvenient. Values say
what you aim at; rules say where the fence is.

The `strategist` skill treats a rule as non-negotiable. If an option on the
table breaks one, it says so rather than weighing it up — that is the point of
writing them down while you are calm.

Keep them few. Ten rules you ignore are worse than three you keep.
