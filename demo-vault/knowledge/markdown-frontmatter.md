# YAML frontmatter conventions

Sample knowledge note. Real vaults collect reference material here — research
results, collected information, the things humans and agents look up rather
than follow.

- Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`, unquoted.
- `tags:` is always a list, even with one entry.
- An empty value is written as a bare key, not as `null`.
