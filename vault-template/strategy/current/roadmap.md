# Roadmap

What you intend to have achieved by when, over the next three to six months.
Short horizon on purpose: a two-year plan cannot be checked against reality
often enough to be worth reviewing.

**Do not duplicate a project's own plan here.** Each project keeps its schedule
in `projects/<project>/roadmap.md` and its dated elements in
`projects/<project>/schedules/`. This note is the layer above: which of them
matter this quarter, and what they add up to. Link, do not copy — two copies of
a date means one of them is wrong.

The `strategist` skill compares this against [[strategy/current/business]] (can
you actually do it with the capacity you have?) and against
[[strategy/north-star/mvv]] (does doing it get you anywhere you want to be?).

This note is seeded once and never overwritten by a template sync — edit it
freely.
